## 📲 How to Install on Android

Installing Shampan Barta on your Android phone takes less than a minute:

1. **Download the APK**  
   Click the [**Download Latest APK**](https://github.com/KamrulHasanSymphony/shampan_barta_releases/releases/latest/download/ShampanBarta_latest.apk) button using your phone's browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, etc.).

2. **Open the File**  
   Once the download completes, tap the notification or open your device's **Downloads** folder and tap `ShampanBarta_latest.apk`.

3. **Allow Installation (If Prompted)**  
   * If Android displays a prompt saying *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   * Tap **Settings** ➔ Toggle on **"Allow from this source"** ➔ Return to the installer.

4. **Tap Install**  
   Confirm by tapping **Install**. Once installed, tap **Open** to launch Shampan Barta!

5. **Log In**  
   Sign in using your regular ERP support staff or client credentials.
